## The QuickBooks Online connection

Kosmos can connect to a firm's QuickBooks Online company, so that the
firm's books can be read in Kosmos while they are kept in QuickBooks.

- The connection is made by an administrator of the firm's Kosmos
  server, signing in to Intuit with the firm's own QuickBooks login and
  consenting to the access. The firm is the party that connects; the
  Kosmos project is not.
- The connection reads. Kosmos copies the company's chart of accounts
  and transactions into the firm's own server and checks them against
  QuickBooks's trial balance. It writes nothing to QuickBooks.
- The firm may disconnect at any time, from Kosmos (Settings →
  Integrations) or from QuickBooks's own list of connected apps. Either
  ends Kosmos's access.
- The firm's use of QuickBooks Online itself is governed by Intuit's
  terms, not by these.
