# Getting started

This page is for attorneys, paralegals and office staff who are opening
Kosmos for the first time. It covers signing in, the page you see first
each day, how the application is laid out, and the settings worth a look
on your first day.

## Sign in

Your administrator gives you the address of your firm's Kosmos, a username
and a password. Kosmos emails you a code every time you sign in, so have
your work email open.

1. Open your firm's Kosmos address. The page reads **Log in to your
   account**.
2. Type your **Username** and **Password** and click **Submit**. The
   username is the one you were given, not your email address.
3. Find the six-digit code in the email Kosmos sends you.
4. Type the code on the next page and click **Verify**.

You are now in Kosmos: on the Dash if this is your first visit of the day,
on **Tasks** otherwise.

To sign out, click **Settings** (it opens on **Session**), then **Logout**.

Good to know:

- The code works for five minutes. After five wrong tries Kosmos discards
  it and shows "Too many incorrect codes. Please log in again."
- There is no button to send the code again. Click **Back to Login** and
  sign in again. That sends a new code and cancels the old one.
- **Logout** signs out the browser you are using, and no other. A browser
  you do not sign out of stays signed in until it goes eight weeks unused.

### If you forget your password

1. Click **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Type the email address on your Kosmos account and click **Submit**.
3. Open the link in the email Kosmos sends (it works for three days), type
   a new password twice and click **Submit**.

Kosmos shows "Your new password has been set." Sign in as usual. You still
need an emailed code.

## Start the day on the Dash

The first time you open Kosmos each day, it takes you to the **Dash**,
whichever page you asked for. After that it leaves you alone until the
next day. The Dash shows up to three sections:

- **Work in Progress**: work that is recorded but not yet billed, as a
  chart and a table of **Hours**, **Gross**, **Comp** and **Net** for each
  matter. It starts on **This Month**. Use the date button on the right to
  pick another period. You see your own work. With the Reports permission
  you see the whole firm's, and can switch between **By User** and **By
  Matter**.
- **Upcoming Events**: the next seven pending events on the matters you
  can see, earliest first. An event whose date has passed stays here
  until it is no longer pending. Click a card to open the event.
- **Collections**: **Past Due** balances and matters with **Low Trust
  Available (Pending)**. Only administrators see this section.

**Plan** opens **Suggested Agenda** in a new browser tab: an AI chat that
drafts an agenda for your day.

Good to know:

- Kosmos checks again in each browser you use, and when you return to a
  tab left open from the day before. It waits while a dialog is open or
  you are typing in a field, then takes that tab to the Dash.

## Find your way around

The sidebar on the left is the main menu. Entries appear in this order.

| Entry | What it is for | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dash** | The day's overview described above. | Everyone |
| **Matters** | The list of matters. Click a name to open one. | Everyone |
| **Calendar** | The firm's events. | Everyone |
| **Tasks** | The firm's to-do list. | Everyone |
| **Contacts** | Clients and everyone else the firm deals with. | Everyone |
| **Activity** | **Time**, **Expenses** and **Flat Fees**. See [Time and expenses](time-and-expenses.md). | Everyone |
| **Library** | Notes, kept in folders. | Everyone |
| **Invoicing** | **Work in Progress**, **Invoices**, **Payments**, **Credits**, **Collection**, **Trust** and **Requests**. | Financial permission |
| **Intakes** | Prospective clients and their intake records. | Intakes permission |
| **Reports** | **Activity**, **Revenue**, **Realization**, **Work in Progress**, **Intakes**, **Clients** and **AR Aging**. | Reports permission |
| **Search** | Opens the **Search** window. See [Search](#search). | Everyone |
| **Settings** | Your profile, appearance, notifications and sign-out. | Everyone |
| **Shortcuts** | Opens the **Keyboard Shortcuts** list. | Everyone |

The button at the foot of the sidebar narrows it to icons, and widens it
again. The Kosmos name at the top opens **Tasks**.

## Work inside a matter

Click **Matters**, then a matter's name, for example "Rivera v. Northside
Logistics". The matter opens on its **Contacts** tab. At the top are the
matter's name, two pills (**Detail** and **Case**) and two small buttons
that add time or an expense to this matter.

A matter has two sides. Click a pill to change sides.

| Side | Tabs |
|---|---|
| **Detail** | **Overview**, **Contacts**, **Rates**, **Activity**, **Events**, **Tasks**, **Proceedings**, **Settlement**, **Ledger** |
| **Case** | **Documents**, **Highlights**, **Timeline**, **Witnesses**, **Notes**, **Emails**, **Labels**, **Search**, **AI**, **Research** |

**Detail** is the office side of the matter: who is involved, what work
was recorded, what is scheduled, the court proceedings and the money.
**Case** is the working file: the documents, the passages you marked in
them, the fact timeline, witnesses, notes, email, and the AI and research
tools. Kosmos reopens each side on the tab you last used there.

**Rates** and **Ledger** need the Financial permission. **Research** needs
the Research permission. Ask your administrator. The tabs are described in
[Matters](matters.md).

### Move to another matter

1. Click the matter's name at the top of the page. A list opens.
2. Click the matter you want.

The other matter opens on the side you were on (**Detail** or **Case**).
On the **Detail** side, the two arrows at the far right step to the
previous and the next matter in the same list.

Good to know:

- The list holds matters with the status **Open** only. To reach any other
  matter, use **Matters** or **Search**.

## Search

1. Click **Search** in the sidebar.
2. Start typing. Results appear as you type, grouped under **Matters**,
   **Proceedings**, **Contacts**, **Intakes** and **Notes**.
3. To narrow the results, click a tab: **All**, **Matters**,
   **Proceedings**, **Contacts**, **Intakes** or **Notes**. (Intakes and
   their tab appear only if you have the Intakes permission.)
4. Click a result.

The matter, contact or intake opens. A note opens in a new browser tab.
Search looks at matter names and descriptions, case numbers, contact and
intake names, email addresses and phone numbers, and notes. A case number
is found without its punctuation: "2024cv12345" finds "2024-CV-12345".

Good to know:

- This search does not read documents. To search inside one matter's
  documents, use the **Search** tab on its **Case** side.
- If you type digits only, Kosmos looks for phone numbers, case numbers
  and a matter's client reference number, not names or notes.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Click **Shortcuts** in the sidebar for a list inside Kosmos, or see
[Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md) for every one. Many start with the
Space bar: press Space, let go, then press the next key within half a
second. These are the ones most worth knowing.

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Space, then `n` | **Create New**: a time entry, task, expense, event, contact or intake |
| Space, then `m` | **Switch Matter**: type part of a name and press Enter |
| Space, then `g` | **Go to**: jump to a sidebar entry or a tab on the page |
| Space, then `f`, `f` | Open **Search** on **All** |
| Space, then `b` | Narrow or widen the sidebar |
| `c` and `d` | Inside a matter, change to the **Case** or **Detail** side |
| `[` and `]` | On a matter's **Detail** side, the previous or next matter |

Good to know:

- Shortcuts do nothing while you type in a field or a pop-up is open.
- **Switch Matter** always opens the matter on its **Detail** side.

## Make it yours

Everything here is under **Settings** in the sidebar.

- **Settings → Profile**: your **Username**, name, **Email address** and
  **Initials**. Click **Update Profile** to save. **Change Password** is
  below it on the same page.
- **Settings → Appearance**: under **Theme**, pick **Auto** (follows your
  computer), a light theme (**Basic**, **Letterhead**, **Matcha**,
  **Nord**) or a dark one (**Everforest**, **Gruvbox**, **Nord**). Under
  **Navigation**, pick **Vertical** (the sidebar) or **Horizontal** (a bar
  across the top). Both apply at once. There is no save button.
- **Settings → Notifications**: click **Enable** beside **Daily Digest
  Email** to get one email a day listing overdue items, today's schedule,
  and events and tasks due in the next three days. **Send Test** sends one
  now.

Good to know:

- Sign-in codes go to the email address on your profile. Changing your
  password does not sign you out.
- The theme is saved in the browser you are using, so set it on each
  device. The navigation choice follows you to every device.
- The digest covers the firm's events and tasks on the matters you can
  see, not only your own. It skips weekends until you click **Mon–Fri Only
  (Enable)**, and sends nothing on a day with nothing to report.

## What you can and cannot see

Your administrator decides what each person can reach, using five
permissions: **All Matters**, **Financial**, **Intakes**, **Reports** and
**Research**. Without **All Matters** you see only the matters you are
assigned to. Without one of the others, its sidebar entries and tabs are
not shown to you. **Search** finds only what you may open. If you follow a
link to a page you may not open, Kosmos shows "You don't have permission
to access this page."

If something you need is missing, ask your administrator. Administrators:
see [Users and permissions](../admin/users.md).
