# Calendar

This page is for anyone at the firm who keeps or checks dates: attorneys,
paralegals and office staff. It covers how to add hearings, deadlines and
appointments, change and find them, and what happens when the firm has
connected a Google Calendar. If you are new to Kosmos, read
[Getting started](getting-started.md) first.

## What an event is

An event is one dated item: a hearing, a deadline, a call, a meeting. It
has one date, a description and a status, and it can have a start and end
time, a matter and a person it is assigned to. An event does not have to
belong to a matter. The same event appears in several places:

- **Calendar** in the sidebar: every event at the firm.
- A matter's **Events** tab: that matter's events.
- A matter's **Overview**: under **Events**, its pending events dated
  today or later.
- The Dash, under **Upcoming Events**. See
  [Start the day on the Dash](getting-started.md#start-the-day-on-the-dash).
- The daily digest email, if you have switched it on. See
  [Make it yours](getting-started.md#make-it-yours).

## Look at the calendar

Click **Calendar** in the sidebar. The two buttons beside the **+** button
switch between **List View** and **Calendar View**. Kosmos opens in the
calendar view and keeps your choice until you sign out. On a narrow
screen, such as a phone, it always shows the list.

### Calendar view

- Click **Month**, **Week** or **Day** at the top right to change the
  span. The arrows at the top left move back and forward, and **Today**
  returns to the current date. Your browser remembers the span.
- Each event reads matter, description, then the initials of the person
  it is assigned to. An event with no start time sits in the all-day row
  of **Week** and **Day**. An event with no matter is drawn lighter.
- Click an event to open **Edit Event**. Click an empty day in **Month**
  to open **Add Event** with that date. Click a date's number to open
  that day in **Day**.
- Drag an event to another day to change its date. In **Week** or
  **Day**, drag it up or down to change its time, or drag its lower edge
  to change when it ends. The change is saved as soon as you let go.

### List view

The list shows 10 events to a page. A pending event dated earlier than
three days from now, including one already past, is highlighted.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The date, and the start time if the event has one. |
| **Matter** | The matter name. Click it to open that matter's **Events** tab. |
| **Description** | Click it to open **Edit Event**. |
| **Duration** | The length in hours, when the event has a start and end time. |
| **Type** | **Zoom**, **Virtual**, **Phone** or **In-person**, if one was chosen. |
| **Party** | **Client**, **Opposing**, **All** or **Other**. |
| **Status** | **Pending**, **Complete** or **Missed**. |

Good to know:

- In **Week** and **Day**, clicking a time slot opens **Add Event** with
  today's date, not the day you clicked, and with no time. Set **Date**
  and **Start time** yourself before you submit.

## Add an event

1. Click **Calendar** in the sidebar, then the **+** button at the left of
   the toolbar. **Add Event** opens.
2. Check **Date**, which starts as today.
3. Choose the **Matter**, or leave it blank for an event that belongs to
   no matter, such as a staff meeting.
4. Enter the **Description**, from 4 to 200 characters.
5. Fill in the other fields that apply and click **Submit**.

The form closes and the event is on the calendar. You can also start from
the **+** button on a matter's **Events** tab or beside **Events** on its
**Overview** (both fill in the matter), or press Space, then `n`, and
choose **Event**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Date** | Yes | An event has one date. It cannot span several days. |
| **Start time** | No | Leave both times blank for an all-day event. |
| **End time** | No | Must be after the start time. If you give a start time only, Kosmos sets the end one hour later. |
| **Assigned to** | No | **Firm** (the default) or one active user. It sets the initials shown on the calendar and what the assignee menu finds. |
| **Matter** | No | Lists the matters that are **Pending** or **Open**. |
| **Description** | Yes | What the event is. |
| **Status** | Yes | **Pending** (the default), **Complete** or **Missed**. |
| **Party** | Yes | Who the event involves: **Client** (the default), **Opposing**, **All** or **Other**. There is no list of attendees. |
| **Type** | No | **Zoom**, **Virtual**, **Phone** or **In-person**. |
| **Location** | No | A meeting link and passcode, or a courthouse address and courtroom. 150 characters at most. |

Good to know:

- On a matter that is **Complete** or **Closed**, the **+** button opens
  **Add Event** with **Matter** blank, because the form lists only pending
  and open matters. An event saved that way belongs to no matter and does
  not appear on the matter's **Events** tab.
- Kosmos has no repeating events. Add each date as its own event.

## Change an event

1. Click the event in the calendar view, or its description in the list,
   on the Dash or on a matter's **Overview**. **Edit Event** opens with
   the same fields as **Add Event**.
2. Make your changes and click **Submit**.

To change only the date or time, you can drag the event in the calendar
view instead.

### Change the status

Set **Status** in **Edit Event**. Kosmos never changes a status for you: a
pending event whose date has passed stays **Pending** until you change it.

| Status | Where the event shows |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Everywhere: the Calendar as it first opens, the matter's **Events** tab and **Overview**, the Dash, the digest and the firm's Google Calendar. |
| **Complete** | On the Calendar and the matter's **Events** tab only when the status menu is on **Complete** or **All Statuses**. It leaves the **Overview**, the Dash and the digest. |
| **Missed** | The same as **Complete**, under its own label. |

### Delete an event

Open **Edit Event**, click **Delete** and confirm. The event is removed
for everyone and cannot be restored.

## Find events

The Calendar opens on pending events, earliest first. The toolbar and the
**Filter** button apply to both views, and Kosmos keeps your choices until
you change them or sign out.

- The status menu chooses **All Statuses**, **Pending**, **Complete** or
  **Missed**.
- The assignee menu chooses **All Assignees**, **Firm** (events assigned
  to nobody in particular), **Firm +** your name (those plus your own), or
  one user.
- The matter menu chooses **All Matters**, **Unassigned** (events with no
  matter) or one pending or open matter.
- Click **Filter** to open **Filter Events**. **Date** takes a first and
  last date. **Party** and **Status** narrow by those fields, and
  **Ordering** sets the sort. Click **Apply**. **Restore Defaults** returns
  to pending events sorted by date.
- In the list, click the sort button beside **Date**, **Matter**,
  **Description**, **Party** or **Status**. Click it again to reverse the
  order.

There is no search box for events, and **Search** in the sidebar does not
find them.

Good to know:

- A date range or party set in **Filter Events** stays on, with nothing in
  the toolbar to show it. If events seem to be missing, click **Filter**,
  then **Restore Defaults**.
- **Matter** and **Assigned to** in **Filter Events** are plain boxes that
  show the toolbar's choice as a number or code. Leave them as they are
  and use the toolbar menus instead.
- In the calendar view, the toolbar menus do not update after **Apply** or
  **Restore Defaults**, and **Restore Defaults** shows every status.
  Reload the page to see pending events and the current choices.

## A matter's Events tab

Open a matter and click **Events**. The tab has the same list and calendar
views for that matter alone, and opens as a list with every event on one
page. The status menu starts on **Pending** and is kept for each matter
separately. The list has no **Matter** column. See [Matters](matters.md).

Good to know:

- **Filter** on this tab opens **Filter Events**, but what you set there
  changes the firm **Calendar**, not this tab. Use the status menu and the
  sort buttons here.

## Work out a deadline

Click **Deadline Calculator** at the right of the toolbar. Enter the
**Start** date and the number of **Days**, then click **Calculate**.
Kosmos shows the **Deadline**, its **Weekday** and, when the deadline
falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the **Next Business Day** (the Monday after
it). The same link is at the top of **Add Event** and **Edit Event**:
there, press Enter in **Days** to calculate.

Good to know:

- The calculator counts calendar days and knows weekends only. It does
  not know court holidays or any court's counting rules.
- It does not add an event. Add the deadline yourself.

## Get reminded of events

Kosmos has no reminders on individual events. Two things bring pending
events to you: **Upcoming Events** on the Dash, and the daily digest
email, which lists pending events under **Overdue**, **Today** and **Next
3 Days**. Both show the firm's events, not only those assigned to you.

## Google Calendar

Your firm can connect one Google Calendar for everyone. An administrator
does this under **Settings → Integrations**. See
[Google Workspace](../admin/integrations/google.md). Until then, **Add
Event** and **Edit Event** say "Not connected to a Google calendar", and
events are kept in Kosmos only.

Once it is connected, events travel both ways:

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Add or change a **Pending** event in Kosmos | Google is updated at once. The title is the matter name, " - ", then the description ("None" stands in for the matter when there is none). The date, the times and the **Location** go too, or the **Type** when there is no location. |
| Mark an event **Complete** or **Missed** | It stays on Google as last sent. Later changes in Kosmos are not sent, and changes on Google are not brought in. |
| Delete an event in Kosmos | It is removed from Google. |
| Add an event on Google | It arrives in Kosmos within about two minutes, assigned to **Firm**. If its title starts with a matter's name and " - ", it joins that matter. |
| Change a synced event on Google | Kosmos takes the new title, date, times and location within about two minutes. |
| Delete a synced event on Google | It is deleted from Kosmos, whatever its status. |

If Google cannot be reached when you save, a warning says the event was
saved but did not sync. Kosmos tries again every two minutes. Ask your
administrator to reconnect if the warning keeps appearing.

Good to know:

- An event added on Google arrives with no status, so it shows only when
  the status menu is on **All Statuses**, and not on the Dash or in the
  digest. Open it, check **Status** and **Party**, and click **Submit**.
- Kosmos finds the matter from the text before the first " - " in the
  Google title, and re-reads its own events this way a couple of minutes
  after sending them. When one matter's name is part of another's, or
  contains " - ", a synced event can move to the wrong matter or lose
  part of its description. Check such events after you save them.
- A repeating event on Google arrives as separate events, and an event
  that spans several days arrives on its first day only.

## Who sees which events

The **Calendar** shows every event at the firm to everyone who can sign
in, and anyone can edit or delete any event there. This is true even if
you are limited to assigned matters (see
[Who can see a matter](matters.md#who-can-see-a-matter)): the Calendar
and the **Matter** list in **Add Event** still include the other matters.

The other places are narrower. A matter's **Events** tab and **Overview**
open only if you can see the matter. The Dash shows events on the matters
you can see and events with no matter. The digest covers the matters you
can see. If you are limited to assigned matters, it leaves out events with
no matter.
