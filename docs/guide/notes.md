# Notes

This page is for anyone at the firm who writes things down: research
outlines, practice guides, interview notes, working memos. It covers the
firm's shared library of notes, the notes kept on a matter, and the editor
you write both in.

## Two kinds of note

| | Library note | Matter note |
|---|---|---|
| Where it lives | **Library** in the sidebar, in folders | The matter's **Case** side, on the **Notes** tab |
| Who can read, change and delete it | Everyone at the firm who can sign in | Everyone who can see the matter (see [Who can see a matter](matters.md#who-can-see-a-matter)) |
| What it is for | Knowledge that outlives one case | Work on that one matter |

## The Library

Click **Library** in the sidebar. **Folders** is on the left and the notes
in the folder you picked are on the right.

- **Inbox** holds library notes that are in no folder. The page starts
  here. **All Folders** lists every library note.
- Click a folder to list its notes. Click the same folder again to return
  to **Inbox**. The arrow beside a folder shows its subfolders.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The note's name. Click it for a menu: **Open**, **Rename**, **Move**, **Delete**. |
| **Author** | Who created the note. |
| **Created**, **Updated** | The dates the note was created and last saved. |
| Flag | The note's importance. Click the icon to set it, from **Highest** to **Lowest**. |

The list shows 20 notes to a page. The sort buttons in the header reorder
it. **Keyword Search** matches note titles only, within the folder you are
looking at.

### Work with folders

1. Click the **+** button beside **Folders**. A form headed **New
   Folder** opens.
2. Choose the **Parent Folder**, or choose **None (root level)** for a
   top-level folder.
3. Enter the **Folder Name** (50 characters at most) and click **Submit**.

The folder appears in the list. Folders nest four levels deep, and two
folders in the same place cannot share a name.

Each folder has a menu, on the three-dot button or by right-clicking the
folder: **New note**, **New subfolder**, **Edit** (name and parent),
**Move** and **Delete**. Right-click **Inbox** for **New note** and **New
folder**. You can also drag a folder onto another folder, or onto
**Inbox** to make it top-level.

**Delete** opens **Delete Folder**. It counts the subfolders and the notes
inside, including the notes in subfolders ("This folder and its subfolders
contain 3 notes."), and asks what to do with them. For an empty folder the
choice is **Cancel** or **Delete**. Otherwise the buttons depend on what
is inside.

When there are notes anywhere inside:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Delete Folder and Notes** | The folder, its notes, every subfolder and the notes in them are deleted. |
| **Delete Folder, Keep Notes** | The folder is removed. Its notes go to **Inbox** and its subfolders move up one level, with their notes. |

When there are subfolders and no notes:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Delete Folder and Subfolders** | The folder and every subfolder are deleted. A note filed into one of them since the dialog opened goes to **Inbox**. |
| **Delete Folder, Keep Subfolders** | The folder is removed and its subfolders move up one level. |

### Work with notes

1. Click the folder the note belongs in (or **Inbox**).
2. Click the **+** button at the top right of the list.

A note named "Untitled" opens in the editor, in a new browser tab. The
folder menu's **New note** does the same.

- **Rename**: click the title, then **Rename**. Change **Title** in
  **Rename Note** and click **Submit**.
- **Move**: click the title, then **Move**, pick a folder (or **Inbox**)
  and click **Move**. Or drag the row onto a folder.
- **Several at once**: tick the boxes at the left. The toolbar then offers
  **Importance**, **Move** and a delete button. Dragging a ticked row
  moves every ticked note.
- **Delete**: click the title, then **Delete**, and confirm.

## A matter's Notes tab

Open the matter, click **Case**, then **Notes**. The tab lists every note
on the matter, most recently updated first. It does not show folders (the
editor does).

- **Add**: click the **+** button. An "Untitled" note opens in the editor
  in a new browser tab.
- **Open**: click the title, then **Open**.
- **Rename**: click the title, then **Edit Details**. **Edit Note** has
  **Matter**, **Category** and **Title**. Click **Submit**.
- **Category**: click the category in the first column to change it:
  **Analysis**, **Drafting**, **Guide**, **Interview**, **Issue**,
  **Note** or **Research**.
- **Label**: click the title, then **Add Label**.
- **Delete**: click the title, then **Delete**, and confirm.

To narrow the list, use the category menu (**All Categories**), the
**Importance** menu (that level and above), **Keyword Search** (titles and
text), or **Filter**, which adds **Label** and **Order By**. An **All
Topics** menu appears when notes on the matter have a topic. The AI can
set a topic when it creates a note.

## The editor

The editor opens in its own browser tab: from **Open** or the **+**
button in either list, from a note in the sidebar **Search**, or from
**Editor** at the top right of the Library, which opens the note you
looked at last (or, when there is no note you can open yet, a page with a
**New note** button).

### Find your way around

The left panel has three tabs, shown as icons: **Library** (the library's
folders and notes), **Matters** (each open matter you can see, with its
own folders and notes) and **Recent** (the last seven notes you opened).
A matter that is not **Open** is listed on **Matters** only while one of
its notes is open in the editor. Click a note to open it in the middle of
the page. Above the list are **New note**, **New folder** (on the
**Library** tab), **Search notes** and **Collapse/Expand all**. The right
panel is the **Outline**: the note's headings, from Heading 2 down. Click
a heading to jump to it. Each panel has a button at its outer top corner
to hide it, and you can drag a panel's inner edge to resize it.

Right-click in the left panel to organise notes without leaving the
editor:

| Right-click on | Menu |
|---|---|
| A folder | **New note**, **New subfolder**, **Edit**, **Delete** |
| A matter | **New note**, **New folder** |
| A note | **Properties** (matter notes only), **Delete** |

A new folder appears as "Untitled" with its name ready to type over. You
can drag a note or folder onto a folder. While you drag, **Move to root**
(or **Move to matter root**) appears at the bottom of the list. To rename
a note, click its title at the top of the note, type, and press Enter.
A title another note in the same folder already has is refused, with a
message saying so.

**New note** above the list follows where you are. On the **Library** tab
it creates the note at the top level of the library. On the **Matters**
and **Recent** tabs it creates the note beside the one you have open: at
the top level of that note's matter, or of the library if the open note
is a library note. To choose where a note goes, right-click the folder or
matter instead.

### Saving

Kosmos saves by itself, two seconds after you stop typing, and again when
you switch notes or close the tab. Press Ctrl+S to save at once. The disk
icon at the left of the toolbar is tinted while there are unsaved
changes. Hover over it to read **Saved**, **Unsaved changes** or
**Saving...**. The line under the title shows when the note was last
saved and by whom.

### Format text

The toolbar has **Undo**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Strikethrough**,
**Heading 2**, **Heading 3**, **Heading 4**, **Bullet List**, **Numbered
List**, **Blockquote**, **Table**, **Insert source** and a highlighter
with a choice of colours. On a narrow window some of these move into the
**More actions** menu (the three dots).

You can also type Markdown and the editor formats it as you go:

| Type | To get |
|---|---|
| `#` to `#####` and a space | Heading 1 to Heading 5 |
| `-` or `*` and a space | A bullet list |
| `1.` and a space | A numbered list |
| `>` and a space | A quote |
| Three backticks and a space | A code block (pick its language at its top right) |
| `---` | A horizontal line |
| `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~struck~~`, `==highlighted==` | That formatting |
| Text between two backticks | Inline code |

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+S | Save now |
| Ctrl+K | Search notes |
| Ctrl+H | Search and replace in this note |
| Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I | Bold, italic |
| Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5 | Heading 1 to 5. Ctrl+0 returns to normal text. |
| Ctrl+Shift+; | Insert source |

On a Mac, use Cmd in place of Ctrl. Saving, the two searches and the
shortcuts list work wherever the cursor is. The other shortcuts act only
while the cursor is in the note's text. **More actions → Keyboard
shortcuts**, or Ctrl+?, lists the rest. See also
[Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md).

In a matter note, **Insert source** searches the matter's highlights and
documents and inserts the one you click as a citation. Click a document
citation to open the document. Click a highlight citation for **View
Detail** and **View Source**. See [Documents](documents.md) and
[Timeline, witnesses and highlights](facts.md).

Good to know:

- The editor has no web links. A Markdown link that is imported becomes
  its text followed by the address in parentheses, and so does one that
  is pasted, the next time the note opens.
- A note is stored as Markdown, so characters that Markdown uses are read
  as formatting the next time the note opens, even where the editor left
  them alone as you typed or pasted. Two asterisks on one line (two
  footnote marks, for example) disappear and the text between them turns
  italic. A line that begins with `#`, `-` or `>` and a space becomes a
  heading, a bullet or a quote. To keep such characters as they are, put
  the text in inline code. Text in angle brackets (an email address
  copied from a message header) and underscores inside a word (a file
  name) are kept as typed. The one exception is `<br>` on its own, which
  becomes a line break (it is how a table cell keeps a second line). A
  Markdown image reference (`![plan](address)`) is kept as typed too; the
  editor shows no images.

### Tables

1. Click **Table** in the toolbar. A second bar appears.
2. Click **Insert table**. A three-by-three table with a header row appears.
3. With the cursor in a cell, use the bar's buttons to add or delete a
   row or column, align a column, switch the header row on or off, or
   click **Delete table**.

You can also type a table: a line such as `| Name | Date |`, then a line
`| --- | --- |`, then Enter. A table pasted as plain text in that form, or
copied from a web page or word processor, becomes a table too. Cells
cannot be merged and columns cannot be resized.

For a second line inside a cell, press Shift+Enter: the line break is
kept. Paragraphs made with Enter inside one cell are joined into a single
line the next time the note opens.

### Import, export and print

**More actions** has **Import from markdown** (upload a file or paste
text, to add to the note or to replace it), **Export to markdown** (a
download named after the note) and **Print** (the note alone, without the
panels).

## Move a note

- **Within the library**: drag it onto a folder, in the Library or in the
  editor, or use **Move** in the Library.
- **Within a matter**: drag it between that matter's folders on the
  editor's **Matters** tab.
- **To another matter**: in the editor, right-click the note and click
  **Properties**. In **Note Properties**, choose the **Matter** and click
  **Move**. Or change **Matter** in **Edit Details** on the Notes tab. The
  note lands at the top level of the new matter. Both lists offer the
  open matters you can see, and the matter the note is on now.
- **Between a matter and the library**: not possible. Copy the text into
  a new note.

Dragging never carries a note or folder from one matter to another.

## Find a note

- In the editor, click **Search notes** or press Ctrl+K. **Search Notes**
  lists your **Recent** notes until you type two characters, then **Title
  matches** (the folder and matter names count too) and **Full-text
  matches** with the words in context. **All**, **Library**, **Matters**
  and, on a matter note, the matter's name narrow the search. Opening a
  full-text match jumps to the words in the note.
- **Search** in the sidebar lists matching notes under **Notes**.
- **Search & Replace** in the editor toolbar (Ctrl+H) works inside the
  open note.

## When two people edit the same note

Kosmos does not merge edits, and it does not show you that someone else
has the note open. The first save wins. If someone else (or the AI) saves
the note while you have changes of your own, your next save is refused
and a banner appears: "This note was changed somewhere else (another tab,
another person or the AI). Editing is paused."

While the banner shows, the note and its title are read-only, and the
disk icon becomes a warning triangle. The formatting buttons, **Import
from markdown**, the Replace buttons and the table bar are hidden until
you reload. You can still select and copy text.

1. Select your unsaved text and copy it.
2. Click **Reload latest**. The note is replaced with the saved version,
   and what you had typed but not saved is gone.
3. Paste your text back where it belongs.

Good to know:

- If you only have the note open to read, it does not refresh when a
  colleague saves. Reload the page to see their changes.

## Notes and the AI

This section applies when your firm has set up AI (see
[AI](settings.md#ai)). Without it, notes are never sent anywhere.

Every library note is offered to the AI on every matter, and a matter's
notes are offered on that matter. There is no setting to hold a note
back. Kosmos writes a short summary of each library note in the
background, which the AI uses to choose the notes worth reading. When you
tell it to in a matter's chat, the AI can create a note on that matter
and add to or rewrite a matter or library note. See
[AI chat](ai-chat.md).

Claude Desktop, if your administrator has
[connected it](../admin/integrations/claude-desktop.md), can read and
search your notes, and can write to a note for 24 hours after you click
the sparkles button in that note's toolbar.

## Deleted notes and earlier versions

!!! note

    Deleting a note or a folder of notes is permanent. There is no
    recycle bin and no version history on the screen, and Undo reaches
    back only through the changes you made since opening the note. Kosmos
    does keep a record of saved versions behind the scenes. If you lose
    text that matters, ask your administrator whether it can be recovered
    from that record.
