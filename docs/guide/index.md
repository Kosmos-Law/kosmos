# User guide

For attorneys and staff who work in Kosmos every day. Each page takes one
part of the application and walks through the things you do there, naming
the buttons and fields as the screen does.

Start with [Getting started](getting-started.md): signing in, the Dash,
finding your way around, search, keyboard shortcuts and your own settings.

Running the practice:

- [Matters](matters.md): finding, opening, running and closing a matter.
- [Contacts](contacts.md): clients and everyone else the firm deals with.
- [Tasks](tasks.md): the firm's to-do list, the board and checklists.
- [Calendar](calendar.md): hearings, deadlines and appointments.
- [Intakes and client forms](intakes.md): prospective clients, from first
  call to client.

Billing:

- [Time and expenses](time-and-expenses.md): recording time, expenses and
  flat fees, and what happens once an entry is billed.
- [Reports](reports.md): what each report shows and how its figures are
  arrived at.

Looking after Kosmos:

- [Settings](settings.md): the firm's details, users, permissions and the
  lists the rest of Kosmos draws on.

Still to be written: invoicing and payments, trust accounting, documents,
the fact timeline and witnesses, research, AI chat, notes and drafts, and
email.

Until those pages exist, two things inside the application help:

- The keyboard shortcut list: **Shortcuts** in the sidebar.
- The setup steps on **Settings → Claude Desktop**, also described in
  [Claude Desktop access to Kosmos](../admin/integrations/claude-desktop.md).
