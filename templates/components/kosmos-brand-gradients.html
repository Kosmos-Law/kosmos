{% comment %}The brand gradients the inline kosmos-wordmark and kosmos-mark cuts fill with (after the landing page's wordmark, mirrored so the brand ink leads): --brand-grad-from to --brand-grad-to, once across the letters and once more on the diagonal across the sparkle, weighted toward the far end. Included once per page (base.html), outside any swapped region. One shared, zero-size SVG rather than defs inside each cut, because the cuts repeat on a page and one copy is always display:none — duplicate ids, and gradients inside hidden subtrees are not reliably referenceable. Coordinates are userSpaceOnUse in each cut's own viewBox, so one gradient spans the whole word instead of restarting per letter. The stops take their colour from the tokens via .brand-grad-from/-to in sidebar.css.{% endcomment %}
