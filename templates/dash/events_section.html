{% comment %} A week, Sunday to Saturday, as a strip of columns: a row of the calendar's month view, today's number circled as it is there. Each column heads with the weekday and, in its upper right, the day of the month, and opens the calendar on that day. Under it the day's pending events, drawn as the month view draws them: an all-day event a filled chip, a timed one a dot, its time and its title; a click opens the event. A day with nothing on it keeps its column, blank; on a phone the strip stacks and empty days drop out. The chevrons swap in the week before or after (apps/dash/week.py), and the card redraws itself when an event changes. {% endcomment %}
