{% comment %} The dash's Calendar Week: a one-week slice of the calendar's month view, Sunday to Saturday, drawn by FullCalendar from dash:week-feed (apps/dash/week.py) through the calendar's own component (events-calendar.js). It works as the month view does: the weekday header row over the days, today's number circled, an all-day event a filled chip and a timed one a dot, its time and its title. An event drags to another day; a click opens it; a click on an empty part of a day adds an event on that day; a day's number opens the calendar on it. With the Tasks toggle on (as it starts), the user's open tasks are on their days, after the events, a box before the title that marks the task done; on the week holding today the overdue ones are gathered on today, dated and in the urgent colour. Past three tasks, a day's others fold into "N more tasks", to the task list. The chevrons step a week; on a phone the week is its agenda. The card refetches when an event or a task changes. {% endcomment %}
