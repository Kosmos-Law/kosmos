{% comment %} A week, Sunday to Saturday, as a strip of columns: a row of the calendar's month view under its weekday header row, today's number circled as it is there. The header row is styled as a table header. Each day's cell has the day of the month in its upper right, which opens the calendar on that day (on a phone, where the header row gives way to a stacked list, the weekday stands before it). Under it the day's pending events, drawn as the month view draws them: an all-day event a filled chip, a timed one a dot, its time and its title; a click opens the event. With the Tasks toggle on (as it starts), the user's open tasks are on their days as the month view has them, a box before the title: the box marks the task done and the rest of the chip opens it. On this week, tasks overdue from before it are gathered at the top of today, dated and in the urgent colour. Past three tasks, a day's others fold into "N more tasks", to the task list. A day with nothing on it keeps its column, blank; on a phone the strip stacks and empty days drop out. The chevrons swap in the week before or after (apps/dash/week.py), and the card redraws itself when an event changes. {% endcomment %}
